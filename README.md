
# 🔥 FIREWATCH
### Satellite Fire Detection Analysis
**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 | Team Area 52**

## Overview

FIREWATCH is a research prototype investigating whether differences between MODIS and VIIRS active-fire detections can provide useful information about satellite fire observations.

## Research Question

Can disagreements between MODIS and VIIRS detections reveal meaningful information about fire observations?

## Objectives

- Explore MODIS and VIIRS active-fire observations.
- Investigate differences between satellite detections.
- Examine spatial and temporal relationships.
- Support transparent and reproducible analysis.

## Application

The web application includes:

- Overview
- Data Explorer
- Comparative Analysis
- Results
- Methodology
- Limitations

## Scientific Integrity

FIREWATCH distinguishes research hypotheses from experimentally observed results.

No quantitative findings will be presented as validated until their source data, processing procedures and outputs have been audited.

**Prototype under development.**

Final experimental results are pending recovery and validation.
